How the weight increases work

Every exercise runs on double progression — the app coaches you through it automatically from the reps you log on the Today tab:

  • You hit the top of the rep range on every set (e.g. told 8–12, you log 12/12/12) → the coach line turns green and tells you the exact new weight for next session (+5 lb dumbbells, +5–10 lb machines, +2.5–5 lb cables).
  • You're inside the range but under the top (told 8–12, you log 10/9/8) → same weight next time; your job is adding reps, not load.
  • You miss the bottom of the range (told 8–12, you log 7s) → same weight, but stop 1 rep earlier on set one so you don't burn out sets two and three.
  • You miss badly (told 8–12, you hit 4) → the weight is simply too heavy: the coach tells you to drop ~10% next session and build back. That's not a failure — finding the right weight IS the first phase.

Set a goal weight on any exercise and the coach also shows how far you are and roughly how many successful weeks it'll take at your increment rate.

When to add weight

Every exercise uses double progression: a fixed set count and a rep range.

  • Pick a weight where you finish each set 1–2 reps shy of failure (reps in reserve).
  • When you hit the top of the rep range on every set with clean form, raise the weight by the increment listed on the exercise (usually +5 lb dumbbells, +5–10 lb machines, +2.5–5 lb cables).
  • After an increase you'll drop back toward the bottom of the range — that's the cycle working.
  • If you miss the bottom of the range two sessions in a row, drop ~10% and build back up.

Rules of the program

  • No squats and no deadlifts — leg day runs through machines (leg press, curls, extensions, hip thrust, abductor/adductor, calves).
  • Lifting 5 days (Mon, Tue, Wed, Fri, Sat); Thursday and Sunday are recovery — 8–10k steps and easy walking.
  • Cardio rides after lifting: 20–30 min incline treadmill or incline walking on the days listed.
  • All major muscle groups are covered, including traps (shrugs, face pulls, carries), forearms (wrist/reverse-wrist curls, reverse curls), obliques (Pallof press), and serratus (cable punch).

Nutrition rules

  • ~2,300 kcal · 140g protein · 295g carbs · 60g fat, split into 4 meals. Vegetarian and gluten-free throughout — oats, tamari, bread, pasta, and granola must be certified GF.
  • The recomp goal (183 → 170–175 lb while building muscle): 2,300 kcal is a ~300–500 kcal deficit at your weight, so expect 0.5–1 lb down per week — that pace reaches 175 in roughly 8–12 weeks while keeping strength climbing. If the scale drops faster than ~1.5 lb/week for two straight weeks, add 100–200 kcal (easiest: more rice or an extra banana + PB) — losing faster costs muscle. If it stalls for 2+ weeks, trim 100–150 kcal. The lifts going up while the weight drifts into the goal zone is the recomp working.
  • ~10g chia most days plus beans/legumes keeps daily fiber in the 30–40g range.
  • Daily non-negotiables: 1 Fairlife 30g shake, creatine, 4 L water, 7–9 hr sleep.
  • Weigh in 2–3× per week under the same conditions; judge trends over 2-week windows, not single days. Adjust calories only if progress stalls.
  • Weekly prep: rice, tofu, roasted potatoes, beans/lentils, chopped vegetables, and 4–5 chia/oat breakfast portions. Keep sauces separate so nothing goes soggy.
